Phill Privacy Choices

Erase local training data

Open Phill → Settings → Data → Erase everything. This removes goals, plans, and workout logs from that device.

Delete the Phill account

Open Phill → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes the active Supabase authentication account and associated profile, Community messages, blocks, and reports. It does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase local training history. If you cannot access the app, submit a deletion request using the private form below. Phill may verify account ownership and processes requests within the period required by applicable law. Limited records may be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, security, and dispute purposes, and residual backup copies expire under provider retention schedules.

Cancel the Apple subscription

Open Phill → Settings → Membership → Manage subscription, or use your Apple Account subscription settings.

Notifications

Change reminders in Phill Settings and notification permission in iOS Settings.

Cloud coach

The cloud coach is off until you enable it. Open Phill → Settings → Coach and turn off Use smarter cloud coach at any time to keep future coach questions on the device.

Community controls

Press and hold a post to report it or block its author. Community is optional.

Privacy request

To ask about access, correction, portability, objection, restriction, or deletion rights available where you live, use Phill’s private report form. Do not send a password, payment details, authentication token, or medical records.
